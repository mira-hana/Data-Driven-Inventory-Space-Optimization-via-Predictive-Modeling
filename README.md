**Data-Driven Inventory & Space Optimization via Predictive Modeling**

*Project Overview*

This project uses data science and machine learning to improve *inventory planning, product profitability, and warehouse space usage*.

The project analyzes **10,000+ retail orders** to identify important factors that affect profit and product performance.

*What I Did*

1) Performed **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** to understand sales, profit, discounts, and product trends.
2) Built **regression models** to predict profit.
3) Built **classification models** to identify profitable and loss-making transactions.
4) Compared models such as **ElasticNet, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting**.
5) Used **Power BI** to visualize sales, profit, product categories, and regional performance.
6) Identified **discount, sub-category, category, and quantity** as important business factors.

*Key Findings*

The analysis found that high sales do not always mean high profit, while large discounts can significantly reduce profit.

The project proposes practical actions such as better discount control, profit-based warehouse slotting, and simpler inventory planning dashboards.

*Tools & Techniques*

**Python • Machine Learning • Power BI • EDA • Regression • Classification • Predictive Modeling**

*Goal*

The main goal is to turn raw business data into **practical insights that can support better inventory, pricing, and warehouse decisions**.
